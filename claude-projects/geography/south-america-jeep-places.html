▼ sources & method
Elevation — ETOPO 20-arcmin global relief (NOAA/NGDC), ~37 km resolution
Climate — Beck et al. 2023, "High-resolution (1 km) Köppen-Geiger maps for 1901–2099," Scientific Data 10, 724, 1991–2020 baseline
Rivers — Natural Earth 10m river & lake centrelines, tinted by stroke rank
Cities — 14 economic capitals, one per country and territory in the box, marked on the minimap and as grey lit-window blocks on the terrain (a second, taller block marks the seat of government). Nine sit on cells the 20-arcmin relief reads as sea; their coordinates are true and land is 15–33 km away, inside the card's radius Three of them — Georgetown, Cayenne and Stanley — sit on cells the 20-arcmin relief reads as sea, because a 26 km cell straddling a coastline averages underwater. Their true coordinates are kept rather than nudged inland; verified nearest dry ground is 22–39 km in each case, inside the 55 km card radius.
Places — 65 named regions from Atlas Drill: South American Climates, resampled onto this lattice. That drill was drawn on Beck 2018 and this field paints Beck 2023, so the two were compared cell by cell: 84.2% of land cells agree class-for-class and 59 of 65 regions keep the same dominant class. 93% of the disagreements are adjacent-class pairs (Aw/Am, Am/Af, BSk/BWk, ET/BWk) — wet- and dry-season thresholds moving under a 26 km cell, not a conflict about where the regions are. The six regions that flip dominant class — the Alto Beni, the Apolobamba Foothills, the Upper and Lower Magdalena, the Tumbesian Dry Forest and the Guajira — flip only to a neighbouring class. The names stand as drawn; the class shown on the card is the one actually painted on that cell.
Shared 205×294 lattice, ~26 km per cell. Relief is 20-arcmin data resampled, so the finer mesh sharpens the coastline (re-cut against a 1/100° Natural Earth land mask) but adds no true relief detail. Class shares on this lattice: Aw 29.0%, Af 17.7%, Am 14.3%, Cfa 9.2%, BSk 5.4%, BWk 4.7%, BSh 4.7%, ET 3.9%.
