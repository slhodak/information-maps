▼ sources & method
Elevation — ETOPO 20-arcmin global relief (NOAA/NGDC), ~37 km resolution
Climate — Beck et al. 2023, "High-resolution (1 km) Köppen-Geiger maps for 1901–2099," Scientific Data 10, 724, 1991–2020 baseline
Rivers — Natural Earth 10m river & lake centrelines, tinted by stroke rank
Cities — 52 economic capitals, one per country in the box, marked on the minimap and as grey lit-window blocks on the terrain (a second, taller block marks the seat of government). Nine sit on cells the 20-arcmin relief reads as sea; their coordinates are true and land is 15–33 km away, inside the card's radius
Places — 66 named regions from Atlas Drill: African Climates. Checked against the climate painted here: 96.7% of land cells and 65 of 66 regions agree, so the names stand as drawn. Only the Afroalpine belt differs, and that is this grid's cell size, not the data vintage.
Shared 319×331 lattice, ~26 km per cell. Relief is 20-arcmin data resampled, so the finer mesh sharpens the painting, not the topography; coastlines are re-cut against a 1/100° land mask.
